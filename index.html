2. Information on your device

Mallang has no account registration or developer-operated cloud sync. 3. Advertising and external SDKs

Mallang uses Google AdMob for advertising. The ads SDK may send IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad/app interactions, performance and diagnostic data, and device identifiers such as advertising IDs to Google and advertising service providers for ad delivery, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean no data is transmitted. Test builds use Google's test ad units. Live advertising checks Google's consent flow and permission to request ads. Where available, privacy choices can be changed on the app's rewards/ad screen; advertising IDs can be reset or deleted in device settings. The developer does not operate a separate analytics server or sell study records.

4. Translation, speech and reminders

Some built-in learning material uses Google ML Kit on-device translation. Translation input and output are processed on the device, but model downloads and SDK diagnostics may send device/app information, installation identifiers, performance and usage metrics to Google. Personal vocabulary is not automatically translated and uploaded. Pronunciation uses device speech synthesis, not microphone recording. Review reminders are scheduled locally after operating-system notification permission. Learning remains available if you decline notifications. The app does not request location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

5. Purchases and support

The remove-ads purchase is available in distributions with store and verification configuration completed. Google Play or Apple processes payment; the developer does not receive card numbers. Purchase status and store transaction/purchase tokens may be used for verification, restoration and fraud prevention, with some entitlement state stored locally. An Android distribution with a configured verification server sends its product ID and purchase token to that server. Real purchasing is not enabled in the current Android test build without that configuration. If you contact us by email, we use your email address and the content you send to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

6. Retention, deletion and security

Local records remain until you delete them. Edit or delete profile information and personal vocabulary in the app. To erase all local app data, clear Mallang's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Manage any operating-system backups separately through your backup provider. There is no account to close. Support messages are kept while needed to handle the request, then deleted unless retention is legally required. External SDK retention and deletion follow the providers' policies and account/device controls. Google SDK transmissions are encrypted; local records use app-specific storage. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

7. Age, external processing and updates

The current distribution is intended for users aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Parents with concerns about a child's information can contact the email above. The age at which legal consent is required may vary by region. Google, Apple and GitHub, which hosts this page, may process information outside your country.
